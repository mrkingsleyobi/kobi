<div class="definition-box">
  <div class="term">Telos</div>
  <div class="desc">Ancient Greek: "end," "purpose," or ultimate aim. The thing everything else is in service of.</div>
  <div class="usage">Used here to mean: what I am working toward, and why.</div>
</div>

<div class="telos-block">
  <div class="telos-label">Missions</div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">M0</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Increase African agency, capability, productivity, security, and prosperity through AI.</strong><span>Africa should not merely consume the AI revolution. It should build with it.</span></div></div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">M1</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Find out how much additional agency one person, one company, or one continent gains when intelligence becomes programmable.</strong><span>Then build the systems that demonstrate it.</span></div></div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">M2</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Measure AI by outcomes, not presentations.</strong><span>The unit of value is an improved outcome.</span></div></div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">M3</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Grow the number of Africans able to build globally competitive AI systems.</strong><span>And a network of capable people around them.</span></div></div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">M4</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Build an institutional ecosystem.</strong><span>A name, a research lab, a consultancy, products, a community, and ventures.</span></div></div>
</div>

<div class="telos-block">
  <div class="telos-label">Current goals</div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">G1</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Get my personal AI infrastructure working by the end of 2026.</strong><span>It should know my goals, projects, knowledge and workflows, route work to the right model, and have agents doing real research, coding, monitoring and analysis.</span></div></div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">G2</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Publish a public body of work by the end of 2026.</strong><span>Research, essays, open source, agent architectures, and security research that show what I build.</span></div></div>
  <div class="telos-item"><div class="telos-id">G3</div><div class="telos-text"><strong>Teach and grow a network of capable African builders.</strong><span>Ongoing.</span></div></div>
</div>
