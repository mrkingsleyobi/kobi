I'm an AI / cybersecurity researcher and founder in Abuja, Nigeria. My work sits between agentic engineering, cybersecurity, and organizational transformation, with Africa as the place I want to prove it.

My loop is simple: research what is actually useful, build it, apply it to real problems, measure what works, and share what I learn. I judge AI work by outcomes, not presentations.

I believe Africa should not just consume the AI revolution. It should build with it, and I want to help more Africans do that.
